Privacy

Privacy policy and terms of use

baseballlineup.ca — Last updated: October 2026 · Version 2.0

1. Who we are

baseballlineup.ca is a web application for managing youth baseball teams, developed and operated in Québec, Canada. It includes the coaches' app and the team page for families (schedule, attendance, chat, files), opened through the link the coach sends them.

Person in charge of the protection of personal information: the founder and operator of baseballlineup.ca. For any question or request, write through the contact form (subject “My data”).

2. Data collected

About coaches (accounts):

  • Name and email address
  • Team name, age group and season

About players (entered by the coach):

  • First and last name, jersey number
  • Positions played and preferred, attendance, innings played and pitched
  • The coach's game evaluations (internal notes, never shown to families)
  • If the team uses the game tracker: at-bat results

About adults who use the team page (parents, coaches):

  • The first name only (no email or phone number is asked of parents) and the children they follow
  • The consents given (policy, photos) and their date
  • For each device: a random identifier (stored hashed), the browser type, the date of the last visit and, if enabled, the notification subscription
  • Attendance replies for games and practices (and who replied)
  • Messages, reactions and photos posted in the chat; files posted by coaches

We do not collect any payment information directly.

3. Minor players and families

Players are generally minors. As such:

  • A team's data is accessible only to its coaches and, for the team page, to people who have its link and password; a coach can remove a person and change the link
  • On the team page, other families only see players' first name and initial
  • A child's stats are shown only to their family (and the coaches), only if the coach enabled it, and never the coach's evaluations
  • A parent can post a photo only after accepting photo sharing; photos are never public (temporary links only) and their location metadata is removed on the phone before upload
  • Private messages connect a family with the whole team staff, never one adult alone with a child
  • No completed medical form should be uploaded: it is handed to the coach in person. Health information (injury, allergy) is written privately to the coaches, not in the team chat
  • Data is never sold or shared for commercial or advertising purposes
  • The coach is responsible for obtaining the consent of parents or guardians to register players in the app

4. Use of data

Data is used exclusively to:

  • Generate lineups and keep the team's schedule, attendance and stats
  • Enable communication between families and the team staff (chat, files, notifications)
  • Ensure the app works properly and securely

No advertising, no resale, no profiling.

5. Hosting and transfer outside Québec

Data is hosted outside Québec, in the United States, by the following providers, who process it on our behalf only:

  • Supabase — database and coach accounts (Virginia, United States)
  • Vercel — hosting of the app and this website (United States)
  • Cloudflare R2 — photos and files (Eastern North America), in private storage
  • Resend — email delivery, such as verification codes and messages from this website (United States)
  • Anthropic — artificial intelligence assistance, only if the team uses it: the information needed for the request (for example first names, positions and evaluations) is sent to produce the answer (United States)
  • Apple, Google or Mozilla notification services, depending on the phone, deliver notifications; their content is end-to-end encrypted

Safeguards:

  • Encrypted communications (HTTPS); data access restricted to each team
  • Device identifiers and verification codes stored only in hashed form
  • Photos and files in private storage, accessible only through temporary links

6. Your rights (Québec Law 25)

Under the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, you can:

  • Access the information about you and correct it
  • Withdraw your consent and have your data deleted

On the team page, you do it yourself, in Plus → Mon profil (More → My profile):

  • Change your first name, the children you follow or your photo consent
  • Leave the team and delete my data: your profile, the children you follow, your devices, your private thread and your photos are deleted immediately; your messages in the team chat remain, without your name (“Former member”)
  • Delete your own messages at any time

For any other request (coaches, a player's data), use the contact form.

7. Data retention

  • Chat, photos and files: deleted automatically 30 days after the end of the season (the last game or practice on the schedule, or the end-of-season date chosen by the coach)
  • A removed photo or file, or an abandoned upload: deleted from storage within 24 hours
  • Devices inactive for a year: deleted; a person with no device left for a year is removed from the team page
  • Verification codes: valid 15 minutes, deleted after one day
  • Questions asked to L'iArbitre (the rulebook assistant) and the "Was this answer helpful?" feedback: kept 12 months to improve answers, then deleted
  • Coach account: kept while active; when deleted, the associated data is deleted within 30 days

8. Cookies and on-device storage

Only cookies needed for the app to work are used, no advertising or tracking cookies:

  • The coaches' sign-in session
  • On the team page, a cookie that recognizes your device (valid up to 400 days, unreadable by the page itself); “Ce n'est pas moi” (“That's not me”) in My profile deletes it
  • The browser's local storage keeps, when needed, a reply waiting for the network and your display preferences (including this website's language)

9. Terms of use

By using baseballlineup.ca, you agree that:

  • The app is provided “as is”, with no guarantee of continuous availability
  • You are responsible for the accuracy of the information you enter and of what you post
  • The chat is for team life: respect, no hurtful comments, no photo of a child without their family's consent; coaches can remove a message or a person
  • Any abusive use or use contrary to sports ethics may lead to suspended access
  • We reserve the right to change these terms with 30 days' notice

10. Changes to this policy

Any significant change is announced to coaches by email and on the team page. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this document.

The French version of this policy prevails in case of discrepancy.
